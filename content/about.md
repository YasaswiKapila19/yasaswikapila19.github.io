I'm Yasaswi. I work as a data scientist at Neuralix.AI, where I build and train large language models. My days go into model architecture, distributed pretraining, and post-training: supervised fine-tuning, preference optimisation, and reinforcement learning. I also spend a fair amount of time making our models run in the tools other people use, like Hugging Face and vLLM.

The work I'm most interested in right now is how architecture choices show up in training and inference, and how far reinforcement learning can push a model's behaviour on real tasks.

## How I got here

I studied chemical engineering at JNTU Hyderabad and, at the same time, did a BS in Programming and Data Science at IIT Madras. After that I spent almost two years at TCS leading a support team for a large enterprise CRM. It taught me how software behaves when real people depend on it. In 2026 I moved to Neuralix.AI to work on language models full time.

Before all of that, I was an NCC cadet at Maharishi Vidya Mandir in Hyderabad.
